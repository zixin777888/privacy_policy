Privacy Policy for Light Refraction (《光之折射》)

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Summary: Light Refraction is an offline single-player puzzle game. We do NOT collect, store, track, or transmit any personal information, device identifiers, or analytics data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Light Refraction ("the Game") does not require an account, internet access, or registration. The Game does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email, phone number, location, or contacts.

2. Permissions Requested

The Game only requests the following permission on your device:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback when rotating optical mirrors or solving a puzzle. This permission does not access any data or files.

3. Local Storage

The Game stores your level progress (such as unlocked levels and star ratings) locally on your device via Android Jetpack DataStore. This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

The Game does not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or external analytics frameworks (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK). There is no third-party data sharing or profiling.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the Game does not collect any personal data, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. It is safe for players of all ages.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact the developer through our project repository at GitHub.
